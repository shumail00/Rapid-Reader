### ⚡ What is Rapid Reader?

Rapid Reader trains your brain to process written information rapidly using **RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation)**. By displaying words sequentially at a fixed visual focal point, eye saccades (the physical movement from word to word) are eliminated—letting you read books and articles up to 3x faster without fatigue.

---

### ✨ Features

* **📖 Multi-Format Document Reader:**
  * **EPUB Engine:** Clean text extraction preserving natural chapter breaks without messy HTML markup.
  * **PDF Engine:** Powered by `PDFBox-Android` with reading-order sorting (`sortByPosition`), preventing broken words and scrambled columns.
  * **Web Article Scraper:** Read directly from online URLs with auto-extracted body content.
  * **Clipboard / Direct Input:** Paste quick notes, scripts, or excerpts on the fly.

* **⚡ RSVP Reading Engine:**
  * **Live Stream View:** Real-time variable speed controls (WPM adjustments on the fly).
  * **Interactive Tap-to-Read:** Tap any word in the static text preview to immediately resume the RSVP stream from that exact spot.

* **📊 Library & Analytics Dashboard:**
  * **Local Library:** Stores your books, tracks reading progress, and remembers where you left off.
  * **Speed Dashboard:** Monitor your reading sessions, personal words-per-minute (WPM) records, and total reading time.

* **🎨 Material You Design:**
  * Fully native Android interface utilizing Material 3 dynamic color theming.
  * Fluid animations and distraction-free dark mode.

* **🔒 100% Private & Local:**
  * Completely offline reading. No analytics, no accounts, and no data tracking.
